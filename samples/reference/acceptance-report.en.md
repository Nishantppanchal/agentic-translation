# Acceptance report – Substation North

*Running header:* Substation North · Project UW-2291 · Confidential

Date: 14 March 2026 | Project no.: UW-2291

## 1. Scope of acceptance

The acceptance covers the renewal of the 110 kV switchgear, including the secondary systems. The work was carried out in accordance with item 4.2.7 of the bill of quantities. The contractor has handed over all test reports.

## 2. Defects found

| No. | Description | Deadline |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cable tray in the basement not earthed | 30 April 2026 |
| 2 | Labelling of the outgoing feeders incomplete | 15 April 2026 |
| 3 | Remaining work on the UPS DC link (intermediate circuit) | 30 April 2026 |

*Figure:* a diagram with three boxes — **distribution board**, **UPS**, **cable tray** — joined by lines, and the note **"Earth connection missing"** at the top.

Fig. 1: Basement overview

## 3. Conclusion

The installation is accepted subject to the rectification of the defects listed above (a conditional acceptance — "with reservations"). The re-inspection is scheduled for week 18. Until then, 5% of the contract sum is retained as security.
