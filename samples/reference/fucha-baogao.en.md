# Defect Re-inspection Report – North District Substation

Date: 20 May 2026 | Project No.: UW-2291

## 1. Scope of Re-inspection

The scope of this re-inspection covers the renovation of the 110 kV switchgear, including the secondary systems. The works were carried out in accordance with item 4.2.7 of the bill of quantities. The Contractor has handed over all test reports in full.

The re-inspection was organised in accordance with the approved Three Measures and One Plan; work on site took place on 20 May 2026 from 9:30 to 14:30. The Client, the Contractor, the construction supervisor and the O&M organisation all sent representatives.

The re-inspection team reviewed the rectification report, the earthing continuity test records and the photographs of concealed works submitted by the Contractor, and checked each of the three defects found at acceptance on site. Where defects had been rectified, the team repeated at least 30% of the measurements on a sample basis; the results agreed with the Contractor's records.

## 2. Defects Identified

| No. | Description | Rectification deadline |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cable tray in the basement not earthed | 30 April 2026 |
| 2 | Labelling of the outgoing feeder bays incomplete | 15 April 2026 |
| 3 | Outstanding work on the UPS DC busbar | 30 April 2026 |

The on-site check found that defect No. 1 has been cleared: a 16 mm² green/yellow copper earthing bonding jumper has been fitted to the cable tray, and the measured earthing continuity resistance is 0.08 Ω, which meets the requirement of no more than 0.2 Ω. Rectification of defect No. 2 is complete: the labels of the outgoing feeder bays are complete and legible. Defect No. 3 has not yet been cleared.

The Contractor shall notify the Client in writing within 5 working days of completing the rectification and request a re-inspection.

Defect No. 3 concerns the UPS DC busbar; the work can only be carried out with the UPS running on bypass and the associated feeders de-energised. According to the monthly outage schedule submitted by the O&M organisation, the period from 7 June to 10 June 2026 is a supply-assurance period for the national college entrance examination (gaokao), during which no planned outages are scheduled. Following consultation, the Client and the Contractor have agreed to extend the rectification deadline for defect No. 3 to 15 June 2026. The Contractor shall revise the Three Measures and One Plan accordingly and, once it has been approved, submit an outage request to the network control centre 3 working days before the outage. Once the outage request has been approved, the O&M organisation will carry out the switching operations and set up the safety measures on site; the Contractor's staff must not operate any primary equipment themselves.

## 3. Conclusion

The equipment acceptance report of 14 March 2026 concluded as follows:

The equipment is granted conditional acceptance pending rectification of the above defects. A re-inspection is scheduled for week 18. Until then, retention money of 5% of the settlement amount will be withheld. De-energisation and re-energisation may only be ordered centrally by the network control centre.

This re-inspection confirms that defects No. 1 and No. 2 have been closed out. With the Client's consent, retention money of CNY 12,500.00 (2% of the settlement amount) will be released now; the remaining 3%, i.e. CNY 18,750.00, will be released once defect No. 3 has been cleared and has passed re-inspection.

The Contractor shall notify the Client in writing within 5 working days of completing the rectification and request a re-inspection.

## 4. Notes

In this report, the “Three Measures and One Plan” means the organisational, technical and safety measures and the construction plan prepared by the Contractor for the outage work; they may only be implemented after review by the O&M organisation and approval by the Client.

## Translator's notes

- **三措一案** (p. 1, twice) is only defined on p. 2 (“本报告所称…”): organisational, technical and safety
  measures plus the construction plan. Rendered “Three Measures and One Plan” throughout.
- **消缺** (defect elimination, utility jargon) is rendered “cleared”; **整改** (termbase) is “rectification”.
  The report uses both for the same defects: a translator may ask whether they differ.
- **运维单位** is the operation and maintenance organisation (here the utility's O&M department, not a
  contractor): “O&M organisation”.
- **保电 / 高考保电期**: a period of guaranteed supply (here during the national college entrance
  examination, gaokao) in which no planned outages are allowed: “supply-assurance period”.
- **监理单位** is the construction supervisor (the independent site supervision firm).
- Amounts: ¥ is written “CNY”. 2% of the settlement amount = CNY 12,500.00, so the settlement amount is
  CNY 625,000.00 and the 5% retention CNY 31,250.00 (the remaining 3% = CNY 18,750.00).
