# Earthing and Insulation Resistance Test Report

On behalf of the Client, the Contractor tested the **main earth grid** in the 110 kV switchgear room of the North District Substation on 18 May 2026. The measured earthing resistance was 0.42 Ω, *which meets the design value of 0.5 Ω*.

## 1. Basis of Testing

The tests were carried out in accordance with the approved [test plan](https://example.com/uw-2291/jiance-fangan) and DL/T 475-2017.[^1]

Before testing, the O&M organisation carried out the necessary switching operations in accordance with the switching order and applied earthing leads on both sides of the equipment under test.

## 2. Test Results

### 2.1 Earthing resistance and earthing continuity

Test conditions: clear weather, ambient temperature 25 °C, relative humidity 45%.

| Measuring point | Measured value | Requirement | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| Main earth grid | 0.42 Ω | ≤ 0.5 Ω | Pass |
| Basement cable tray | 0.08 Ω | ≤ 0.2 Ω | Pass |
| UPS cabinet | 0.35 Ω | ≤ 0.2 Ω | Fail |

### 2.2 Insulation resistance

Test conditions: clear weather, ambient temperature 25 °C, relative humidity 45%.

| Measuring point | Measured value | Requirement | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 110 kV switchgear panel, phase A | 2500 MΩ | ≥ 1000 MΩ | Pass |
| 110 kV switchgear panel, phase B | 2300 MΩ | ≥ 1000 MΩ | Pass |
| 110 kV switchgear panel, phase C | 2400 MΩ | ≥ 1000 MΩ | Pass |

## 3. Conclusion

The earthing continuity resistance of the UPS cabinet exceeds the limit, so the cabinet is assessed as failed. The Contractor must clear the defect and repeat the test by 15 June 2026.

All other test items passed; the equipment concerned is ready to be put into service.
