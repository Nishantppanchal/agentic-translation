# Extract from the Construction Contract – Substation North

Contract No. UW-2291-BV of 12 January 2026 | Project No.: UW-2291

The following extract reproduces the provisions of the construction contract between the Client and the Contractor for the renewal of the 110 kV switchgear at Substation North that govern the execution of the works. The VOB/B, in the version applicable when the contract was concluded, applies in addition.

## § 4 Time for Completion

(1) The Contractor shall commence the works on 2 February 2026 and complete them, ready for acceptance, by 13 March 2026. Both dates are binding contractual deadlines within the meaning of § 5 VOB/B. No later than 10 working days before the start, the Contractor shall submit to the Client a construction schedule agreed with the network control centre.

(2) If the Contractor considers that it is being hindered in the proper execution of the works, it shall notify the Client without delay (notice of hindrance under § 6(1) VOB/B). If it fails to give notice, it is entitled to have the hindering circumstances taken into account only if the Client was clearly aware of the facts and of their hindering effect.

(3) The notice must be given in writing and addressed to the Client's project management.

## § 5 Concerns About the Method of Execution

(1) If the Contractor has concerns about the intended method of execution, about the quality of the materials or components supplied by the Client, or about the work of other contractors, it shall inform the Client without delay (notice of concerns under § 4(3) VOB/B).

(2) If the Client maintains its instruction despite a properly submitted notice of concerns, the Contractor is not liable for any defects resulting from it.

(3) The notice must be given in writing and addressed to the Client's project management.

## § 6 Remuneration and Payment

(1) The remuneration for the works under LV position 4.2.7 is a lump sum of EUR 1,248,500.00 net, plus statutory VAT, currently 19%.

(2) Interim payments shall be made within 21 calendar days of receipt of a verifiable interim invoice. The final payment is due no later than 30 days after receipt of the verifiable final invoice.

(3) If the Contractor culpably exceeds the deadline under § 4(1), it shall pay a contractual penalty of 0.2% of the net contract sum for each working day of delay, but no more than 5% of the net contract sum in total.

## § 7 Acceptance

(1) Formal acceptance shall take place once the works have been completed; deemed acceptance under § 12(5) VOB/B is excluded. The Contractor shall give written notice of completion. Acceptance shall take place within 12 working days of the Client's receipt of the notice of completion. A joint record of acceptance shall be drawn up, stating any defects and contractual penalties that are reserved and any objections raised by the Contractor.

(2) Risk passes to the Client on acceptance. The warranty period begins on the day of acceptance and lasts 5 years.

## § 8 Security

(1) As security for claims for defects, the Client shall retain 5% of the final invoice total (security retention). The Contractor is entitled to replace the security retention with an absolute guarantee (without the defence of prior recourse) from a credit institution authorised in the European Union.

(2) Any part of the security retention that has not been drawn upon shall be returned when the warranty period expires, but no later than 31 March 2031.

## Annex 1 – Terms and Abbreviations

Client (AG): Acme Energie Netz GmbH, represented by Dipl.-Ing. M. Weber.

Contractor (AN): Albrecht Elektrotechnik GmbH, represented by K. Albrecht.

VOB/B: German Construction Contract Procedures (Vergabe- und Vertragsordnung für Bauleistungen), Part B, 2016 edition.

LV: the Client's bill of quantities (Leistungsverzeichnis), as of 5 December 2025.

## Translator's notes

- **AG / AN** are used from the first paragraph but only defined in Annex 1 on p. 2: Auftraggeber (AG) =
  the Client, Auftragnehmer (AN) = the Contractor. Rendered “the Client” / “the Contractor” throughout
  (the abbreviations are kept only in Annex 1, where they are defined).
- **Abnahme** (termbase: acceptance) is a legal act under German construction law: *förmliche Abnahme* =
  formal acceptance, *fiktive Abnahme* = deemed acceptance; *abnahmereif* = ready for acceptance.
- **Behinderungsanzeige** (§ 6(1) VOB/B) = notice of hindrance (also “notice of obstruction/delay”).
- **Bedenkenanzeige** (§ 4(3) VOB/B) = notice of concerns (the contractor's written reservations about the
  specified method, materials or prior work).
- **Sicherheitseinbehalt** (termbase: security retention); *selbstschuldnerische Bürgschaft* = an absolute
  guarantee, enforceable without first pursuing the principal debtor.
- **VOB/B** and **LV** are kept as German abbreviations (as in the project style guide: codes unchanged).
- Amounts: 1.248.500,00 EUR → EUR 1,248,500.00 (decimal point in English).
